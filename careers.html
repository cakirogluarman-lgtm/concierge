What we look for

Skills are teachable. These are not.

You show up

Our customers pay for reliability. If you say you'll be there at 9, you're there at 9. No-shows aren't a thing here.

You love dogs

You'll meet a hundred of them. Bring treats. Know how to read body language. If a dog doesn't trust you, you don't belong in this role.

You sweat the details

You'll notice when a gate is latched wrong, when a pup looks under the weather, when the trash bin is overflowing. Care is in the details.

Roles we'll be hiring for

Listed in the order we'll be hiring. All positions are W-2 (not gig) with paid mileage and uniforms.

🐾

Field Technician

Full-time · Opening summer 2026 · WPB area

The role at the heart of our service. You'll drive a route of 8–14 yards per day, clean them to our spec, photograph the work, and treat every customer like they're your only one. Pay starts at $22/hr plus mileage, with a fast track to lead technician.

📞

Customer Experience Lead

Part-time → full-time · Opening late 2026 · Remote-friendly

Answer texts and emails. Solve problems before they become ones. Make every customer feel like they have a person on speed-dial. If you've ever wished a service company had treated you better, this is your chance to build that company.

🗺️

Dispatch & Routing

Part-time · Opening when we hit 200 members · WPB area

Optimize our daily routes, juggle weather reroutes, handle last-minute add-ons. You like puzzles. You like making people's day easier. Software-savvy a plus.
