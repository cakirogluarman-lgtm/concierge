About us

A new standard for the world's least glamorous service.

The Yard Concierge was built on a simple idea: pet waste removal should feel as reliable and easy to track as anything else you pay for in 2026 — your groceries, your rideshares, your dog's vet visits.

"

We're building the most trustworthy yard service in Palm Beach — one where every visit is logged, photographed, and confirmed in real time. No no-shows. No guessing. No surprises.

What we stand for

Three commitments that shape every visit, every yard, every interaction.

Trust by default

Every technician is background-checked, trained, and uniformed. We're fully insured. We photograph the gate behind us. We text when we arrive and when we leave. Nothing about your yard service should require a phone call.

Tracking that's actually useful

You'll know exactly when we're coming, who's coming, and what they found. Visit history, photo proof, schedule changes — all in one place. No more wondering if it got done.

Care for the small things

We double-bag every scoop. Sanitize tools between yards. Replace divots in the lawn. Bring a fresh bowl of water if your dog comes outside. The little things compound — and they're what make a yard service feel like a luxury.
